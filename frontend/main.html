How the AI Avatar System Works

Six intelligent steps from photo to fully styled 3D avatar.

01

Photo Capture

The system captures a live photo using your camera or lets you upload an image for processing.

02

AI Face Analysis

AI detects facial features and identifies face shape — oval, round, square, diamond, and more.

03

Gender & Model Selection

Based on gender and face shape, the system automatically selects the most suitable 3D avatar model.

04

3D Rendering

Three.js renders the avatar in real time with lighting, rotation, zoom, and camera controls.

05

Virtual Try-On

Users can apply dresses and hairstyles on the avatar for an interactive virtual fashion experience.

06

Future Enhancements

Planned: realistic skin textures, AI outfit recommendations, AR try-on, and full animation support.
